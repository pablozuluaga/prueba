# Preset: Accounting, Tax & Bookkeeping Firms — Metro Manila, Philippines

Sell-to fit: video-meeting platforms, client portals, document-collection tools, e-signature,
practice-management software. Offshore-client work makes video routine here.

## Location filter

Metro Manila. Areas: Makati, BGC/Taguig, Ortigas (Pasig/Mandaluyong), Quezon City, Alabang,
Pasay, Manila (Binondo/Ermita), San Juan.

## Lead categories

1. Accounting and bookkeeping firms serving overseas clients (AU/US/UK outsourcing)
2. Tax consultancies (BIR compliance) with online consultation
3. Business-registration and corporate-services firms
4. Audit and advisory boutiques
5. Payroll/HR outsourcing providers

## Mention terms

English (business language): online consultation, virtual meeting, video call, Zoom meeting,
book a consultation, remote bookkeeping, serving clients worldwide, online appointment.

Tagalog: rarely used for professional services — English-first market.

## Priority rubric

- High: offshore-client positioning ("we serve Australian/US businesses") — video meetings are
  their delivery channel; published consultation booking; active LinkedIn.
- Medium: local-client firms with "online consultation available."
- Low: contact-form-only firms.

## Exclusions

Big 4 and top-10 networks (enterprise IT); solo freelancers on marketplaces only.

## Market notes

- The offshore-accounting sector (thousands of firms) meets foreign clients by video daily
  across time zones — tool quality and recording are real pains.
- Facebook pages are common even for professional firms; LinkedIn for the offshore segment.
- BIR deadlines (April) spike consultation demand — good outreach timing.
