# Preset: Business & Management Consultancies — Johannesburg, South Africa

Sell-to fit: video-meeting platforms, proposal/e-signature tools, client portals, workshop
software (load-shedding-resilient tools are a real differentiator).

## Location filter

Johannesburg. Areas: Sandton, Rosebank, Midrand, Randburg, Fourways, Braamfontein, Bryanston,
Illovo, Melrose Arch.

## Lead categories

1. Strategy and management consultancies (SMB-focused)
2. BEE/transformation advisory firms
3. Financial and tax advisory boutiques
4. HR and skills-development consultancies (SETA-linked training)
5. Business coaches and franchise consultants

## Mention terms

English (business language): virtual consultation, online consultation, virtual meeting, video
call, virtual workshop, webinar, online session, book a consultation, Teams/Zoom meeting,
serving clients across Africa.

## Priority rubric

- High: pan-African client positioning (cross-border video is their delivery mode); SETA-
  accredited training providers running virtual courses; published booking links.
- Medium: local firms with "virtual meetings available."
- Low: brochure sites without consultation productization.

## Exclusions

Big-name strategy firms (enterprise procurement); dormant shelf-company consultancies.

## Market notes

- Load-shedding made resilient, low-bandwidth video a stated pain — a differentiator generic
  tools don't address; name it in outreach.
- SETA accreditation registers are verification cross-checks for training providers.
- LinkedIn dominates professional visibility; response rates improve via referral-style openers.
