# Preset: Coaches & Independent Consultants — Sydney, Australia

Sell-to fit: video platforms, scheduling/booking tools, client-portal and course software,
payment tools. High tool-spend willingness; solo-to-small teams decide fast.

## Location filter

Greater Sydney. Areas: CBD, North Sydney, Parramatta, Surry Hills, Chatswood, Bondi Junction,
Manly, Newtown, Sutherland Shire, Hills District.

## Lead categories

1. Executive and leadership coaches
2. Business coaches for SMBs/tradies
3. Financial advisers and mortgage brokers with online reviews/meetings
4. Health and mindset coaches with online programs
5. Fractional consultants (CFO/CMO/HR) serving national clients

## Mention terms

English: online sessions, virtual sessions, video call, Zoom sessions, online coaching, virtual
consultation, book a discovery call, work with clients Australia-wide, online program.

## Priority rubric

- High: productized discovery-call funnels (Calendly + published packages); "Australia-wide" or
  international client positioning; group-program cohorts run by video.
- Medium: local-only coaches with online option.
- Low: hobby pages without booking or pricing signals.

## Exclusions

MLM-style "coaches"; directory-only profiles without owned web presence.

## Market notes

- Time-zone spread (Perth to NZ clients) makes async recording + scheduling features
  concretely valuable.
- Discovery-call funnels are near-universal — booking links are easy High-priority evidence.
- LinkedIn + Instagram split by segment: executive coaches on LinkedIn, health/mindset on
  Instagram.
