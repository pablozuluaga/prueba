# Preset: Coaching & Exam-Prep Institutes — Delhi NCR, India

Sell-to fit: live-class platforms, LMS, doubt-solving tools, test-series software, student CRMs.

## Location filter

Delhi NCR. Areas: Mukherjee Nagar (UPSC hub), Old Rajinder Nagar (UPSC), Karol Bagh, Laxmi
Nagar (CA/commerce hub), South Extension, Kalu Sarai/Hauz Khas (IIT-JEE), Dwarka, Rohini,
Janakpuri, Noida Sector 18/62, Gurgaon (Sector 14, DLF), Ghaziabad.

## Lead categories

1. UPSC / state-services coaching institutes
2. IIT-JEE / NEET coaching centers
3. CA / CS / commerce coaching
4. SSC / banking / government-exam academies
5. IELTS and spoken-English institutes
6. Online-first academies headquartered in NCR

## Mention terms

English: online batch, live classes, online course, hybrid batch, recorded + live, online test
series, doubt sessions online, app-based classes.

Hindi: ऑनलाइन क्लास, ऑनलाइन बैच, लाइव क्लास, ऑनलाइन कोर्स, ऑनलाइन तैयारी
(also Roman Hindi on social: "online batch shuru", "live class roz").

## Priority rubric

- High: runs daily live batches with published timetables and online fee payment; thousands of
  enrolled students; own app or portal (they still buy better live infrastructure).
- Medium: hybrid institutes with occasional online doubt sessions.
- Low: single "online admission open" mention with no visible class operation.

## Exclusions

National ed-tech giants' sales pages (Unacademy/BYJU'S-scale — different buying motion);
franchise landing pages without an NCR operating center.

## Market notes

- Mukherjee Nagar and Old Rajinder Nagar institutes moved heavily to hybrid post-2020 and
  publish batch PDFs — rich mention sources.
- Laxmi Nagar CA coaching is dense, price-sensitive, and WhatsApp-first.
