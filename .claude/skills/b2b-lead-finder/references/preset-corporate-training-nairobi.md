# Preset: Corporate Training & Professional Development — Nairobi, Kenya

Sell-to fit: webinar/virtual-classroom platforms, LMS, certificate tools, event registration.

## Location filter

Nairobi. Areas: Westlands, Upper Hill, CBD, Kilimani, Karen, Parklands, Gigiri (UN/NGO belt),
Lavington, Industrial Area.

## Lead categories

1. Corporate training and management-development firms
2. Professional certification providers (PMP, ACCA, CPA, HR)
3. NGO/development-sector training organizations
4. Leadership and executive-coaching consultancies
5. ICT and digital-skills academies

## Mention terms

English (primary): online training, virtual training, webinar, virtual workshop, e-learning,
online course, virtual classroom, remote training, hybrid training, Zoom training.

Swahili: mafunzo mtandaoni, masomo mtandaoni — appears occasionally; English dominates
professional training.

## Priority rubric

- High: published training calendars with virtual delivery option and fees; NGO-sector trainers
  running regional (multi-country) virtual programs — East Africa reach makes video their core.
- Medium: "we also deliver online" without calendars.
- Low: legacy in-person outfit with one pandemic-era mention.

## Exclusions

Universities (different procurement); one-person motivational speakers without recurring programs.

## Market notes

- Gigiri/Westlands NGO training market runs constant regional webinars — bandwidth-tolerant,
  low-data video tools are a differentiator worth naming.
- LinkedIn is strong for Kenyan professional-services; training calendars live on websites.
- M-Pesa payment links near booking forms signal a productized (High-priority) operation.
