# Preset: Course Creators & EdTech Academies — Mexico City, Mexico

Sell-to fit: live-class/webinar platforms, LMS, payment tools (MSI installments), community
platforms, funnel software.

## Location filter

Mexico City (CDMX). Areas: Polanco, Condesa, Roma Norte, Del Valle, Nápoles, Santa Fe,
Coyoacán, Insurgentes corridor, Satélite (Edomex fringe on request).

## Lead categories

1. Online course academies (marketing, finance, tech) running live cohorts
2. Professional certification and diplomado providers
3. Language academies with online groups
4. Business/entrepreneurship bootcamps
5. Creative-skills schools (design, photography) with live online classes

## Mention terms

Spanish: clases en línea, curso en línea, clases en vivo, diplomado online, taller virtual,
webinar, sesiones en vivo, inscripción en línea, curso con clases en vivo, asesoría virtual.

English: rarely — Spanish-first market.

## Priority rubric

- High: cohort-based programs with published start dates and "clases en vivo"; diplomado
  providers with enrollment flows; active webinar funnels (registration pages).
- Medium: recorded-course sellers (upsell live components).
- Low: single "cursos en línea" mention.

## Exclusions

Marketplace-only instructors (Udemy/Domestika catalog sellers); public universities' extension
programs.

## Market notes

- "En vivo" is the qualifier separating live operations from recorded catalogs.
- Instagram + YouTube for marketing; WhatsApp for enrollment conversations; webinar funnels
  (Zoom/Meet registration pages) are strong High-priority evidence.
- MSI (meses sin intereses) payment mentions signal real revenue operations.
