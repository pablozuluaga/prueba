# Preset: Dental & Aesthetic Clinics — Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

Sell-to fit: teleconsultation platforms, clinic booking systems, patient CRMs, secure video
(patient privacy is a strong angle), WhatsApp-business tooling.

## Location filter

Riyadh. Areas: Olaya, Al Malaz, Al Nakheel, Al Sulimaniyah, King Fahd District, Al Rawdah,
Al Murabba, Diplomatic Quarter (DQ), Al Yasmin, Exit 5 / King Abdulaziz Road corridor
(Al Ghadir / Ar Rabi).

## Lead categories

1. Dental clinics and dental centers
2. Aesthetic / dermatology / cosmetic clinics
3. Polyclinics offering online booking or remote follow-up
4. Orthodontic and pediatric dental specialists
5. Medical centers advertising teleconsultation

## Mention terms

English: online consultation, teleconsultation, virtual consultation, online booking, remote
follow-up, video consultation.

Arabic: استشارة عن بعد، استشارة أونلاين، حجز أونلاين، عيادة افتراضية، استشارة عبر الفيديو،
متابعة عن بعد

## Priority rubric

- High: active teleconsultation offering (bookable video visits, published telehealth hours) or
  multi-branch clinics coordinating specialists remotely; active Instagram/Snapchat presence.
- Medium: online booking only (no video care yet) — upsell path to teleconsultation.
- Low: a stale "we offer remote consultation" line with no booking route.

## Exclusions

Hospital groups with in-house enterprise IT (different procurement); aggregator listings
(Vezeeta-style platforms) rather than the clinics themselves.

## Market notes

- Saudi telehealth regulation (SFDA/MoH licensing) pushed clinics toward compliant video tools —
  compliance/privacy is a door-opener.
- Instagram and Snapchat matter more than Facebook in Riyadh; clinic bios carry WhatsApp links.
- Arabic pages are the norm; English mentions cluster in DQ/Olaya premium clinics.
