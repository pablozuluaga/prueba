# Preset: Fitness, Yoga & Wellness Coaching — Dubai, UAE

Sell-to fit: live-class streaming platforms, booking/membership systems, client-coaching apps,
payment tools.

## Location filter

Dubai. Areas: Dubai Marina, JLT, Business Bay, Downtown, Jumeirah (1-3), Al Barsha, JVC,
Al Quoz (studio district), DIFC, Mirdif.

## Lead categories

1. Yoga and pilates studios with online class options
2. Personal-training businesses with remote coaching
3. Nutrition and wellness consultancies (online consultations)
4. Corporate-wellness providers running virtual sessions
5. Ladies-only fitness providers (online demand is structural)

## Mention terms

English: online classes, virtual classes, live online sessions, Zoom classes, online coaching,
virtual PT, online consultation, train from home.

Arabic: تدريب أونلاين، حصص أونلاين، استشارة أونلاين — English dominates Dubai fitness marketing.

## Priority rubric

- High: published online class schedules or paid remote-coaching programs; corporate-wellness
  providers (recurring B2B webinars/sessions); Ramadan-season online program history (annual
  recurring demand).
- Medium: "online options available" without schedules.
- Low: gym with a single lockdown-era post.

## Exclusions

Global app platforms; hotel gyms; one-off influencer workout pages without paid programs.

## Market notes

- Instagram is the storefront — schedules in stories/highlights, booking via bio links or
  Classpass; WhatsApp for PT inquiries.
- Expat churn means clients keep training with Dubai coaches after relocating — remote coaching
  is a durable revenue line coaches want to professionalize.
- Summer (Jun-Sep) moves fitness indoors/online — seasonal outreach window.
