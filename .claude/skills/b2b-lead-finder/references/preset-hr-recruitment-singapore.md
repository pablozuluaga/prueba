# Preset: HR Consultancies & Recruitment Firms — Singapore

Sell-to fit: video-interview platforms, meeting tools, assessment software, client/candidate
CRMs. Regional (APAC) remit makes video the default meeting mode.

## Location filter

Singapore. Areas: CBD/Raffles Place, Tanjong Pagar, Orchard, Bugis/City Hall, Paya Lebar,
Jurong East, Novena, Harbourfront.

## Lead categories

1. Recruitment and executive-search firms (regional APAC desks)
2. HR consultancies (compensation, org design) with virtual workshops
3. Corporate-training and leadership-development providers
4. Employer-of-record / relocation firms (cross-border by nature)
5. Career-coaching and outplacement services

## Mention terms

English (business language): virtual interview, video interview, online consultation, virtual
meeting, webinar, virtual workshop, remote hiring, regional clients, book a call.

Chinese: 线上面试, 在线咨询 — occasionally on bilingual firms; English first.

## Priority rubric

- High: regional positioning ("APAC coverage", "clients across Southeast Asia") — daily
  cross-border video; published webinar/workshop calendars; MOM-licensed employment agencies
  (license number displayed — also your verification source).
- Medium: local-only boutiques with occasional virtual meetings.
- Low: static brochure sites.

## Exclusions

Global staffing giants (enterprise IT); job boards.

## Market notes

- MOM's public employment-agency register is the legitimacy cross-check.
- LinkedIn is the dominant channel — most mention-evidence lives in LinkedIn posts and event
  pages rather than websites.
- PDPA compliance + candidate-data confidentiality is a workable security angle.
