# Preset: IELTS & Test-Prep Centers — Karachi, Pakistan

Sell-to fit: video/class hosting platforms, LMS, student CRMs, payment tools, mock-test software.

## Location filter

Karachi metro. Areas: Gulshan-e-Iqbal, DHA, Clifton, North Nazimabad, PECHS, Bahadurabad,
Saddar, Tariq Road, Johar (Gulistan-e-Johar), Nazimabad, Korangi, Malir.

## Lead categories

1. IELTS / PTE / TOEFL prep centers
2. Study-abroad consultancies that run their own prep classes
3. Spoken-English and language academies
4. O/A-Level and university-admission coaching centers
5. Online-only test-prep academies based in Karachi

## Mention terms

English: online class, online batch, live class, online IELTS preparation, Zoom class,
online session, virtual class, online course, evening online batch, recorded lectures.

Urdu: آن لائن کلاس، آن لائن کورس، آن لائن تیاری، لائیو کلاس، آن لائن بیچ
(Facebook pages here mix Roman Urdu — also search: "online class hoti hai", "online parhai",
"ghar bethe tayari").

## Priority rubric

- High: published online batch schedules (dates, fees, timings) or a booking/enrollment form for
  online classes; multiple batches per month; active Facebook (weekly posts).
- Medium: says "online classes available" without schedules; hybrid centers where online is
  secondary.
- Low: one old post about online classes from exam-season past.

## Exclusions

Franchise HQ pages without a Karachi campus; visa agents who only refer students elsewhere for
prep; YouTube-only tutors with no enrollment.

## Market notes

- Prep centers cluster around Gulshan-e-Iqbal and Tariq Road; DHA/Clifton centers charge more
  and adopt tools faster.
- Facebook is the primary channel (more than websites); WhatsApp numbers are usually in the
  page About or pinned post as wa.me links.
