# Preset: Immigration Consultants — Toronto (GTA), Canada

Sell-to fit: video-consultation platforms, booking/payment systems, document-collection portals,
client CRMs. Clients are overseas — video IS the business.

## Location filter

Greater Toronto Area. Areas: Brampton, Mississauga, Scarborough, North York, Etobicoke,
Downtown Toronto, Markham, Vaughan.

## Lead categories

1. RCIC-licensed immigration consultancies
2. Immigration law firms with consultation booking
3. Student-visa / study-abroad advisories
4. Settlement and work-permit specialists
5. Citizenship-test prep and settlement-services firms

## Mention terms

English: online consultation, virtual consultation, book a consultation, video consultation,
Zoom consultation, phone/video appointment, consultation fee, worldwide clients.

Punjabi/Hindi (GTA client base): ਆਨਲਾਈਨ ਸਲਾਹ, ऑनलाइन परामर्श — mostly appears as English even on
Punjabi-language pages; search Punjabi/Hindi page names + "online consultation".

## Priority rubric

- High: paid video consultations with online booking + fee published (the standard motion here);
  overseas client base stated ("clients in India/Philippines/Nigeria"); RCIC number displayed.
- Medium: free phone consultations only.
- Low: form-only contact, no consultation productization.

## Exclusions

Unlicensed "agents" (verify RCIC via the CICC public register — also your cross-check source);
firms outside GTA serving Toronto only nominally.

## Market notes

- This vertical already sells consultations by video across 10+ time zones — you are upgrading
  their tool, not creating a behavior. Recording + secure document sharing resonate.
- Brampton/Mississauga firms market heavily on YouTube and Instagram reels; booking links in bio.
