# Preset: Language Schools & Exam Prep — Istanbul, Turkey

Sell-to fit: live-class platforms, LMS, enrollment/payment tools, speaking-practice products.

## Location filter

Istanbul. Areas: Kadıköy, Beşiktaş, Şişli/Mecidiyeköy, Beyoğlu/Taksim, Bakırköy, Üsküdar,
Levent, Ataşehir, Bahçelievler.

## Lead categories

1. English language schools (dil kursu) with online groups
2. IELTS/TOEFL/YDS exam-prep centers
3. Turkish-for-foreigners schools (online demand from abroad)
4. German/French course providers (emigration-driven demand)
5. Online-first language academies based in Istanbul

## Mention terms

Turkish: online ders, online kurs, canlı ders, çevrimiçi eğitim, online eğitim, uzaktan eğitim,
online grup dersi, birebir online ders, ücretsiz deneme dersi online.

English: online class, live lesson — secondary; Turkish first.

## Priority rubric

- High: online group schedules with enrollment ("kayıt") flows; "canlı ders" (live lesson)
  operations with published timetables; trial-lesson booking.
- Medium: hybrid schools mentioning online options.
- Low: single "online eğitim" line, no schedule.

## Exclusions

App-only products (Duolingo-style); university language centers (different buyer).

## Market notes

- Emigration wave (German/English demand) created heavy online-group supply — growth segment.
- Instagram is the storefront; DM/WhatsApp for enrollment; websites carry schedule tables.
- "Canlı" (live) is the qualifier that separates real live-class operations from video libraries.
