# Preset: Law Firms & Legal Consultancies — Lagos, Nigeria

Sell-to fit: secure video meeting platforms (client confidentiality angle), e-signature,
practice-management and client-intake tools.

## Location filter

Lagos. Areas: Victoria Island, Ikoyi, Lekki Phase 1, Ikeja (incl. GRA), Yaba, Surulere,
Apapa, Festac, Ajah.

## Lead categories

1. Corporate/commercial law firms
2. Immigration and citizenship-by-investment practices
3. Property/real-estate law and land-documentation firms
4. Tax and regulatory-compliance consultancies
5. Solo/boutique chambers advertising virtual consultations

## Mention terms

English (primary language of business): virtual consultation, online consultation, virtual
meeting, video conference, book a consultation online, remote consultation, virtual law clinic,
e-consultation, Zoom meeting, Teams meeting.

## Priority rubric

- High: paid consultation booking online (Calendly/forms with fees), diaspora-focused practices
  (immigration, property for Nigerians abroad — video is their core channel).
- Medium: "virtual meetings available on request."
- Low: generic contact page, no consultation productization.

## Exclusions

Bar-association directories (use as verification source, not leads); firms with no Lagos office.

## Market notes

- Diaspora clients (UK/US/Canada) drive video-consultation adoption — property and immigration
  firms mention time zones and "wherever you are in the world."
- LinkedIn and firm websites are the mention-rich sources; Instagram for boutique firms.
- Confidentiality/data-protection (NDPR) is a resonant security pitch.
