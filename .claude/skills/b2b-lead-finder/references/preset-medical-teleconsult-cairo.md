# Preset: Medical Specialists & Teleconsultation — Cairo, Egypt

Sell-to fit: telehealth video platforms, clinic booking systems, patient-record tools,
payment collection.

## Location filter

Greater Cairo. Areas: Nasr City, Maadi, Zamalek, Heliopolis (Masr El Gedida), Mohandessin,
Dokki, New Cairo (Fifth Settlement), 6th of October City, Garden City, Shubra.

## Lead categories

1. Specialist private clinics (dermatology, psychiatry, pediatrics, obgyn) with online visits
2. Mental-health clinics and counseling centers (largest online segment)
3. Nutrition/dietician practices with online follow-ups
4. Polyclinics and medical centers advertising remote booking
5. Gulf-facing practices (Egyptian doctors consulting Saudi/Gulf patients remotely)

## Mention terms

Arabic: كشف أونلاين، استشارة أونلاين، استشارة عن بعد، حجز أونلاين، كشف عن طريق الفيديو،
متابعة أونلاين، عيادة أونلاين

English: online consultation, teleconsultation, video visit — Arabic first for this market.

## Priority rubric

- High: bookable online كشف (visit) with published fees; psychiatry/nutrition practices running
  weekly online follow-ups; Gulf-patient positioning (regional remote income stream).
- Medium: WhatsApp-based informal follow-ups.
- Low: single pandemic-era mention.

## Exclusions

Aggregator platforms (Vezeeta — verification source, not a lead); public hospitals.

## Market notes

- Facebook is the dominant clinic channel in Egypt; fees and schedules are posted openly —
  quote-rich. WhatsApp booking is near-universal (wa.me links everywhere).
- Egyptian specialists monetize Gulf-patient teleconsults at premium rates — better video
  infrastructure is directly revenue-linked for them.
- Evening hours (8pm-1am) are clinic prime time; reflect in activity checks.
