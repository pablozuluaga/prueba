# Preset: NGOs & Development-Sector Training — Dhaka, Bangladesh

Sell-to fit: webinar/meeting platforms (data-sovereignty angle for donor compliance), LMS,
event-registration tools. Companion to the Convay Dhaka preset — this one goes deep on the
NGO/development segment only.

## Location filter

Dhaka. Areas: Gulshan, Banani, Baridhara (donor/INGO belt), Mohakhali, Dhanmondi, Mirpur,
Agargaon, Motijheel, Uttara.

## Lead categories

1. National NGOs running training/capacity-building programs
2. INGO country offices with regular virtual convenings
3. Research institutes and think tanks hosting webinars
4. Professional associations with member webinars
5. Donor-funded project units (time-bound but budgeted)

## Mention terms

English: webinar, virtual workshop, online training, virtual convening, online dialogue,
hybrid event, capacity-building online, virtual launch event.

Bangla: অনলাইন প্রশিক্ষণ, ওয়েবিনার, ভার্চুয়াল কর্মশালা।

## Priority rubric

- High: organizations with recurring webinar series (monthly dialogues, annual virtual
  conferences), multi-district programs coordinated from Dhaka (field-office video calls), or
  donor-reporting webinars; NGO Affairs Bureau registration (verification cross-check).
- Medium: occasional event-based webinars.
- Low: one-off pandemic-era webinar archives.

## Exclusions

Dormant NGOs (no activity in 12 months); briefcase NGOs without verifiable offices.

## Market notes

- Data sovereignty resonates: donor compliance + government-adjacent programs prefer locally
  hosted, controllable platforms over foreign clouds — decisive differentiator vs Zoom.
- Webinar evidence lives on Facebook events and LinkedIn; reports (PDFs) name their platforms.
- Budget cycles follow donor fiscal years (Jul-Jun common) — time outreach to planning windows.
