# Preset: Convay — Dhaka, Bangladesh

Campaign configuration for finding qualified B2B leads in Dhaka for **Convay**
(convay.com), a secure video conferencing and collaboration platform. Load this when the user
asks for Convay leads or Dhaka online-service leads; copy its structure for new campaigns.

## Product capabilities to sell against

Secure online meetings · class/webinar hosting · session recording · file sharing ·
client consultation calls · large-meeting support · enterprise security / data control
(strong angle for clinics, legal/tax firms, NGOs handling sensitive conversations).

## Location filter

Dhaka, Bangladesh only. Name the specific area per lead — Dhanmondi, Gulshan, Banani, Uttara,
Motijheel, Mirpur, Bashundhara, Farmgate, Mohammadpur, Panthapath, Badda, Khilgaon, Paltan,
Karwan Bazar, Mohakhali, Tejgaon, Lalmatia, Shyamoli, Wari, Baridhara.

## Lead categories

1. Coaching centers and education institutes
2. IELTS, language, and admission coaching centers
3. Corporate training providers
4. Online course providers
5. Consultancy firms (business/management)
6. Legal, tax, business, immigration, and student consultancy firms
7. Clinics, doctors, and telemedicine/online consultation providers
8. NGOs, associations, and training organizations
9. Professional service firms that run online meetings with clients

## Priority notes specific to Convay

- **High**: recurring online classes or consultations at scale — IELTS batch programs, telemedicine
  platforms with booking systems, training institutes with published online course calendars,
  NGOs running regular webinar programs. These need hosting + recording + security weekly.
- **Medium**: hybrid providers where online is a secondary channel.
- **Low**: one-off webinar mentions, dormant pages.
- Security/data-sovereignty angle ranks clinics, legal/tax consultancies, and NGOs up one notch
  when they handle sensitive client conversations online — Convay's differentiation vs Zoom/Meet.

## Known exclusions

- Businesses outside Dhaka metro (including "Dhaka Division" towns like Gazipur/Narayanganj
  unless the user opts in).
- Pure content publishers (YouTube channels, blogs) with no client sessions.
- Platforms that resell courses but run no live sessions.
