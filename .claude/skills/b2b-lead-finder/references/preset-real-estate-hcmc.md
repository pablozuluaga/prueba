# Preset: Real Estate Agencies — Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

Sell-to fit: video-tour/meeting platforms, CRM, listing tools, e-signature. Overseas-buyer
segment (Viet Kieu + foreign investors) makes video consultations core.

## Location filter

Ho Chi Minh City. Areas: District 1, Thao Dien / An Phu (former District 2, now part of Thu Duc
City — expat hub), District 3, District 7 / Phu My Hung (Korean/international), Binh Thanh
(Landmark 81 area), District 4, Go Vap, Thu Duc City (wider — university/new-development belt).

## Lead categories

1. Agencies serving foreign buyers/renters (English/Korean/Japanese-speaking)
2. New-project sales agencies (căn hộ/condo launches) with online sales events
3. Property-management firms with remote owner reporting
4. Serviced-apartment and leasing agencies (video viewings standard)
5. Real-estate consultancies for overseas Vietnamese (Viet Kieu) investors

## Mention terms

Vietnamese: tư vấn trực tuyến, tư vấn online, xem nhà online, video call xem nhà, họp trực
tuyến, livestream mở bán, đặt lịch online.

English: online viewing, virtual tour, video call viewing, online consultation, remote purchase
support for overseas buyers.

## Priority rubric

- High: overseas-buyer positioning with video consultation/viewing offered; livestream project
  launches ("livestream mở bán" — sales events by video); English/Korean pages.
- Medium: agencies mentioning video viewings on request.
- Low: listing-only pages with phone contact.

## Exclusions

Individual freelance brokers on marketplace sites; developers' corporate HQs.

## Market notes

- Zalo (not WhatsApp) is the messaging channel — capture Zalo numbers/links as the
  whatsapp-equivalent field.
- Facebook is the dominant business platform; livestreamed sales events are common and are
  strong High-priority evidence.
- Viet Kieu remittance-driven property buying = year-round remote-consultation demand.
