# Preset: SaaS & Digital Agencies — Berlin, Germany

Sell-to fit: video/meeting platforms (GDPR angle is strong), project-management and client-portal
tools, sales/CRM software, async-collaboration products.

## Location filter

Berlin. Areas: Mitte, Kreuzberg, Prenzlauer Berg, Friedrichshain, Charlottenburg, Schöneberg,
Neukölln, Moabit, Adlershof (tech park).

## Lead categories

1. Digital/web agencies running client workshops remotely
2. B2B SaaS startups doing remote demos and onboarding
3. UX/design studios with remote discovery sessions
4. IT consultancies and system houses (Systemhäuser)
5. Marketing agencies serving DACH clients remotely

## Mention terms

English: virtual meeting, online workshop, remote demo, video call, online onboarding, webinar.

German: Online-Beratung, virtuelles Meeting, Online-Termin, Videokonferenz, Online-Workshop,
Webinar, Remote-Beratung, Erstgespräch online, Termin buchen.

## Priority rubric

- High: bookable "Erstgespräch" (intro call) links, published webinar calendars, remote-first
  client process described on site; 10-100 employees (big enough to pay, small enough to switch).
- Medium: "remote möglich" mentions without productized booking.
- Low: single contact form, no visible remote motion.

## Exclusions

Pure freelancers (one-person, no recurring meeting volume); enterprise consultancies (procurement
cycles too long for this motion).

## Market notes

- GDPR/data-residency is THE door-opener in Germany — "DSGVO-konform" as a differentiator against
  US tools appears constantly in their own marketing; mirror it.
- LinkedIn + firm websites are mention-rich; Xing still matters for Systemhäuser.
- Berlin agencies publish Calendly/HubSpot booking links — easy productized-meeting evidence.
