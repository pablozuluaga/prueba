# Preset: Telemedicine & Clinics — Jakarta, Indonesia

Sell-to fit: telehealth video platforms, EMR/booking systems, patient-communication tools,
secure messaging.

## Location filter

Jakarta proper (default): SCBD/Sudirman, Kuningan, Menteng, Kemang, Kelapa Gading, Pondok
Indah, Kebayoran Baru, Pluit, Tebet. Jabodetabek extension (only if the user opts in):
BSD/Tangerang, Bekasi, Depok.

## Lead categories

1. Klinik (GP clinics) advertising online consultation
2. Specialist practices (obgyn, pediatric, dermatology) with telekonsultasi
3. Mental-health clinics and psychologists offering online sessions
4. Homecare / lab services with remote doctor review
5. Aesthetic clinics with virtual pre-consultations

## Mention terms

English: online consultation, teleconsultation, telemedicine, video call with doctor.

Indonesian: konsultasi online, telekonsultasi, konsultasi dokter online, layanan daring,
janji temu online, konsultasi via video call, periksa online.

## Priority rubric

- High: bookable telekonsultasi with published fees/hours; multiple doctors; active Instagram
  (Indonesian clinics live on Instagram).
- Medium: WhatsApp-based informal consultation (upgrade path to structured video).
- Low: pandemic-era mention, no current booking route.

## Exclusions

Super-app telehealth platforms (Halodoc, Alodokter) — they are competitors/channels, not leads;
hospital chains with corporate IT procurement (heuristic: 4+ branches or membership in a named
group like Siloam, Mayapada, Hermina — multi-doctor single-site clinics still qualify).

## Market notes

- Instagram bio + linktree is where booking links live; WhatsApp numbers near-universal.
- Kemang/Pondok Indah/SCBD clinics serve expats — English pages and premium pricing.
- Ministry of Health telemedicine rules (post-2023) make compliant platforms an easy pitch.
