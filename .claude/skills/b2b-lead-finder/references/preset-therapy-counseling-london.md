# Preset: Therapy & Counselling Practices — London, UK

Sell-to fit: secure video platforms (confidentiality is the purchase driver), booking systems,
client-notes tools, payment processing.

## Location filter

London. Areas: Harley Street (private-practice hub), Islington, Camden, Richmond, Clapham,
Shoreditch, Kensington, Hampstead, Canary Wharf, Wimbledon.

## Lead categories

1. Private therapy and counselling practices (individual and group)
2. Psychotherapy clinics with associate teams
3. Couples/family therapy centers
4. Corporate wellbeing/EAP providers
5. Coaching-plus-therapy hybrid practices

## Mention terms

English: online therapy, online counselling, video sessions, remote sessions, telehealth,
online appointments, sessions via Zoom, secure video, work with clients worldwide.

## Priority rubric

- High: practices with associate teams (5+ therapists) offering online sessions — they buy
  centrally; published online-booking with session fees; "online and in-person" as standing offer.
- Medium: solo therapists with online sessions (small deal size but high volume).
- Low: directory-profile-only presence (no owned booking).

## Exclusions

NHS services (public procurement); directory aggregators (Psychology Today — use as a
verification source, not a lead).

## Market notes

- Confidentiality/GDPR/BACP ethics drive tool choice — "secure, GDPR-compliant video" is the
  pitch, and many are uneasy on generic Zoom. Strongest security-angle vertical in this library.
- Online-first became permanent post-2020: most practices publish both modes.
- BACP/UKCP registries are cross-check sources for legitimacy.
