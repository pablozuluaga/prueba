# Preset: Tutoring & Cursinhos — São Paulo, Brazil

Sell-to fit: live-class platforms, LMS, student payment/enrollment tools, mock-exam software.

## Location filter

São Paulo city. Areas: Paulista/Jardins, Pinheiros, Vila Mariana, Moema, Itaim Bibi, Tatuapé,
Santana, Lapa, Santo Amaro, Mooca.

## Lead categories

1. Cursinhos pré-vestibular (university-entrance prep, ENEM/FUVEST)
2. Concursos públicos prep courses (civil-service exams)
3. Language schools (English/Spanish) with online groups
4. Reforço escolar / tutoring centers
5. Online-first course providers based in SP

## Mention terms

Portuguese: aula online, aula ao vivo, curso online, turma online, plantão de dúvidas online,
aulas remotas, curso EAD ao vivo, matrícula online, monitoria online, aula particular online,
simulado online.

English: rarely used — search Portuguese first.

## Priority rubric

- High: published online turmas with schedules and matrícula (enrollment) flows; live doubt
  sessions ("plantão ao vivo"); active Instagram with class reels.
- Medium: recorded-only EAD courses (upsell: add live components).
- Low: "aulas online disponíveis" with no visible operation.

## Exclusions

Mega-platforms (Descomplica-scale); franchised language chains' national HQ pages (target the
unit/franchise pages instead).

## Market notes

- Instagram + WhatsApp dominate; cursinhos publish schedule PDFs — quote-rich sources.
- "Ao vivo" (live) is the key qualifier separating live-class operations from recorded EAD.
- LGPD (Brazil's GDPR) gives a privacy angle for minors' classes.
