terms, city area lists, a vertical-specific priority rubric, exclusions, and market notes.
Match the user's product to the **Sell-to fit** column, then load the preset. For uncovered
markets, copy `preset-template.md` and fill it from the nearest neighbor.

## Reuse rules

- A preset for the same vertical in a different city transfers ~80% — swap area lists, adjust
  local-language terms, check which platform dominates locally (Facebook, Instagram, Zalo,
  WhatsApp, LINE…).
- Priority rubrics encode what "recurring online activity" looks like in that vertical; keep the
  logic, adapt the thresholds.
- Presets may extend the output columns with market-specific channels (e.g. `zalo`, `line`) —
  see the Output columns section of SKILL.md.
