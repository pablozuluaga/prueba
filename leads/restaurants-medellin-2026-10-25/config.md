# Campaign: Restaurants in Medellín that need a website / app

Seller: web + app development (restaurant website, digital menu, online ordering / reservations, delivery app).

## Location filter
Medellín, Antioquia, Colombia (municipality). Areas: El Poblado, Laureles–Estadio, Envigado border (flag as metro), Belén, La América, Centro, Robledo, Buenos Aires, Manila, Provenza, La 70, Florida Nueva, San Javier/Comuna 13.
Metro-area towns (Envigado, Sabaneta, Itagüí, Bello) are accepted only if the row says so in notes.

## Qualifying signal (why this is not literal "they posted a want ad")
Restaurants rarely post "we need a website". The evidence of need is public, quotable behaviour:
- orders/reservations taken by hand: "pedidos por WhatsApp", "escríbenos al interno / DM", "domicilios al 3xx…", "reservas por WhatsApp"
- menu published only as photos / PDF / Instagram highlights ("menú en historias destacadas", "carta en el link")
- link-in-bio (Linktree, wa.me) instead of an own domain; or a dead / placeholder website
- explicit asks: "buscamos desarrollador web", "necesitamos página web / app" (bonus, rare)

## Lead categories
1. Restaurantes de comida típica / corrientazo / menú del día
2. Comidas rápidas, hamburgueserías y dark kitchens (domicilios)
3. Cafés, panaderías y brunch
4. Restaurantes de especialidad (pizza, sushi, mexicana, árabe, vegana)

## Mention terms
Spanish: "pedidos por WhatsApp", "pide por WhatsApp", "domicilios", "escríbenos al interno", "pedidos al DM", "reservas por WhatsApp", "menú del día", "carta digital", "link en bio", "pide aquí", "necesitamos página web", "buscamos desarrollador web".
English: "order via WhatsApp", "DM to order", "menu in highlights".

## Priority rubric (replaces the generic one)
- High: active, takes orders/reservations manually every day (domicilios / reservas por WhatsApp or DM) AND has no own website (or a broken one).
- Medium: has only a link-in-bio / marketplace page (Rappi, Didi) / PDF menu, or a dated basic site; or an explicit request with little activity.
- Low: single or passing mention, unclear volume.

## Exclusions
Chains with corporate sites and their own apps (Crepes & Waffles, El Corral, Frisby…), restaurants with a working ordering website, aggregator listings without a findable business, closed businesses.

## Target
25 verified leads (probe run), 3-round cap.
